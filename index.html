The lights went out on the home world long ago. Your family's debt grows every day and the bank won't wait.

With your last money you bought a rusty mining station at auction that had stood in the Ochre desert for years. Every few days comets fall from the sky, and with them come raw diamonds, which still pays back on the home world.

Legend says the desert is home to the Stoneheart, a giant whose nest grows stones worth enough to wipe out any debt. You're not the only one who knows. And the others aren't friendly.
